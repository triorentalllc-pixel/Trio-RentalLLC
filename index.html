FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you rent.

Can I pay with a debit card? +

Yes, we accept both credit and debit cards. A security deposit will be held on your card at the time of pickup and released upon return of the vehicle.

Is insurance included in the rental price? +

Basic liability coverage is included. We recommend using your personal auto insurance or your credit card’s rental coverage for full protection. Additional coverage options are available at pickup.

Can I return the car to a different airport? +

Yes! One-way rentals between our three airports (DCA, BWI, IAD) are available. Please mention this when booking so we can arrange it for you.

What happens if the car breaks down? +

Call us immediately at (571) 488-8483. We provide roadside assistance and will arrange a replacement vehicle as quickly as possible at no extra charge.

How do I pick up my car at the airport? +

After booking, we’ll send you exact pickup instructions via email or text. Our team will meet you at the designated meeting point at your airport with your vehicle ready to go.

Is there a mileage limit? +

We offer unlimited mileage on all rentals within the DC, MD & VA area. Long-distance trips outside the region may have additional conditions — contact us to discuss.
